Quick Reference

The high-yield facts from Blood, Part 2.

Three-step hemostasis

1) Vascular spasm 2) Platelet plug (primary) 3) Coagulation/fibrin (secondary).

Coagulation

Extrinsic + intrinsic → common pathway → prothrombin activator → thrombin → fibrin. Thrombin is the big switch.

Anemia vs polycythemia

Anemia = low O₂-carrying capacity. Polycythemia = excess RBCs → viscosity → clot risk.

ABO rule

Antigen = what you ARE; antibody = what you ATTACK. O = universal donor, AB = universal recipient.

Thrombosis

Thrombus (in place) → embolus (travelling) → embolism (lodged). Risk: injury, stasis, hypercoagulability.

Bleeding pattern

Platelet problem → petechiae/skin bleeding. Factor problem → deep joint/muscle bleeding.

Layla at altitude: chronic low O₂ → EPO → secondary polycythemia. Helpful in moderation; hyperviscosity and clot risk in excess.
