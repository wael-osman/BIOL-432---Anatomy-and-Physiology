Quick Reference

The high-yield facts from Blood, Part 1.

Composition

Plasma ~55% · buffy coat <1% (WBCs+platelets) · red cells ~45% = hematocrit (♂47±5, ♀42±5).

Hemoglobin

4 globin chains (2α+2β) + 4 heme; 1 Hb carries 4 O₂. ♂13–18, ♀12–16 g/dL.

Erythropoiesis

Kidney senses low O₂ → EPO → marrow. ~2 million red cells made per second; lifespan 100–120 days.

Differential (N L M E B)

Neutrophils 50–70% · Lymphocytes 25–45% · Monocytes 3–8% · Eosinophils 2–4% · Basophils 0.5–1%.

WBC jobs

Neutrophil→bacteria; Eosinophil→parasite/allergy; Basophil→histamine; Lymphocyte→adaptive; Monocyte→macrophage.

The spleen

Quality-control filter for aging red cells (recycles iron) and an immune-surveillance organ.

Layla's labs (Hb 10.4, low ferritin, small pale cells) = iron-deficiency anemia in an endurance athlete: the marrow has the EPO signal but lacks iron to build hemoglobin.
