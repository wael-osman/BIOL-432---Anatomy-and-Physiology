# BIOL 432 — Anatomy & Physiology

Interactive self-check activities for **BIOL 432 (Anatomy & Physiology)** at Khalifa University, College of Medicine and Health Sciences.

Each activity runs entirely in the browser — no login, download, or install. They work on phone, tablet, and laptop, and open directly from the course hub.

**▶ Live hub:** https://wael-osman.github.io/biol432/

## Activities by week

| Week | Topic | Activities |
|------|-------|-----------|
| 1 | Orientation & body language | The Language of the Body |
| 2 | Body fluids & tissues | Tonicity Self-Check · Tissue Self-Check |
| 3 | Tissues & the integument | Tissue Detective · Skin Explorer |
| 4 | Nervous system I | Neuron Navigator · Signal Lab |
| 5 | Nervous system II | Synapse & Autonomic Studio · Special Senses Studio |
| 6 | Musculoskeletal system | Muscle Contraction Explorer · Bone Explorer |
| 7 | Endocrine system I | Endocrine Signaling Lab · Thyroid & Calcium Clinic |
| 8 | Endocrine system II | Endocrine Detective · HPG Axis & the 28-Day Cycle |
